# Hineni: questions for a rav

Hineni (https://maxatrilion1.github.io/Hineni/) is a Jewish clock that shows where you stand in the hour, the day, the week, the month, the year, the shemitah cycle and the six thousand years. Every statement in it now names its source. Where we could not source something to the letter, it is either removed or, if listed here, marked in the app with a small **?**, until a rav has looked at it.

Each question says what Hineni does now, what we found, and what we are asking.

---

## A. Things Hineni shows now, marked with ?

### 1. Each hour paired with a month and its tribe
**Now:** the 1st hour of the day is paired with Nissan (and Yehudah), the 2nd with Iyar (and Yissachar), through the 12th with Adar (and Naftali). The night repeats the same twelve. Each hour then shows that month's letter, sense, mazal and tribe.
**Found:** Tanya (Chinuch Katan, before Sha'ar HaYichud VehaEmunah): "בכל שעה שולט צירוף אחד מי״ב צירופי שם הוי״ה בי״ב שעות היום, וצירופי שם אדנ״י בלילה". The months are also paired with the twelve permutations of the Name. We found no source that pairs the 1st hour with Nissan's permutation, the 2nd with Iyar's, and so on, and Tanya gives the night hours permutations of a different Name.
**Questions:** Is there a source that pairs each hour of the day with a month (and so with its tribe)? If so, which, and in what order? What should the night hours show?

### 2. A "guest" (ushpizin) for each day of the week
**Now:** Sunday Avraham, Monday Yitzchak, Tuesday Yaakov, Wednesday Moshe, Thursday Aharon, Friday Yosef, Shabbos David, alongside the sefirah of each day.
**Found:** the sefiros of the weekdays are from the Arizal (Pri Etz Chaim, Sha'ar HaMoadim 3). The ushpizin are a Sukkos teaching (Zohar III 103b). We found no source that gives them to the weekdays. The Zohar, Hashmatos to vol. II (276a-b), pairs these figures with the days of creation in a different order (4th day David, 5th Moshe, 6th Aharon, Yosef with Shabbos).
**Questions:** Should Hineni show a figure for each weekday at all? If so, by which source and order?

## B. Choices between shitos (Hineni uses one; is it right to?)

### 3. Zmanim
**Now:** hours from sunrise to sunset (the Alter Rebbe's Siddur), dawn at 16.1°, nightfall at 6°, sunrise and sunset at sea level. The app says that Chabad.org computes some times differently (dawn at 16.9°, "true" sunrise and sunset) and to follow one's shul's luach.
**Questions:** For a Chabad-based clock, should Hineni switch to Chabad.org's method exactly (16.9° and netz/shkiah amitiyim)? Is the Magen Avraham option (dawn to nightfall) worth keeping?

### 4. The months and the tribes
**Now:** the order of the camps' marching (Bamidbar 10:14-27): Nissan Yehudah, Iyar Yissachar, Sivan Zevulun, Tammuz Reuven, Av Shimon, Elul Gad, Tishrei Ephraim, Cheshvan Menashe, Kislev Binyamin, Teves Dan, Shevat Asher, Adar Naftali. The Gra (on Sefer Yetzirah 5:3) and the Bnei Yissaschar bring this in the name of the Arizal.
**Found:** the Gra there also brings an order "from Chazal" that includes Levi and counts Yosef as one tribe (Sivan Naftali, Tishrei Yosef, Cheshvan Levi, Adar Zevulun).
**Question:** Is the order of the camps the right one to use?

### 5. Sefer Yetzirah: letters, senses and mazalos of the months
**Now:** the Gra version, 5:7-10. Nissan ה speech, Iyar ו thought, Sivan ז walking, Tammuz ח sight, Av ט hearing, Elul י action, Tishrei ל union (tashmish), Cheshvan נ smell, Kislev ס sleep, Teves ע anger, Shevat צ eating, Adar ק laughter.
**Found:** the standard printed version lists the senses in another order and does not pair them with the letters.
**Question:** Is the Gra version the right one to follow?

### 6. Stones of the choshen
**Now:** as Bamidbar Rabbah 2:7 pairs them, which is also where the flags' colors come from.
**Found:** Rashi (Shemos 28:21) and Targum Yonasan pair the stones with the tribes in birth order instead.
**Question:** Keep Bamidbar Rabbah's pairing?

### 7. Kiddush Levanah
**Now:** from 7 days after the molad (Shulchan Aruch 426:4) to half a month after it (Rema 426:3); the app notes that the Rema allows from 3 days, that the Mechaber gives 15 days for the end, and to ask one's rav.
**Question:** What should Hineni show by default for Chabad practice (for example, from the start of the 7th day)?

### 8. The planet of each hour
**Now:** each hour shows its planet in the order שצ״ם חנכ״ל (Rashi, Berachos 59b), with its traits from Shabbos 156a. The source notes that Rabbi Yochanan says "אין מזל לישראל".
**Question:** Is it appropriate to show this, and in this way?

## C. Rabbi Miller's suggestions (not added yet)

### 9. The meaning of the letters
**Plan:** for each month's letter, what Sefer Yetzirah says of it (the letter "made king" over the sense, forming the mazal, the month and a part of the body), and perhaps the teaching of the letters in Shabbos 104a.
**Question:** Which sources would you want used for the meaning of each letter?

### 10. The meaning of the tribes
**Plan:** for each tribe, the meaning of its name and of Yaakov's and Moshe's blessings, from the verses with Rashi (Bereishis 29-30, 35:18, 41:51-52, 49; Devarim 33).
**Question:** Is Rashi the right basis, or another commentary?

### 11. The angels of the days and the hours
**Found:** the planets of the hours are in the Gemara and Rashi (above). Lists of an angel for each day (for example Michael for Sunday, Gavriel for Monday) appear in Sefer Raziel and similar works. We could not confirm a mainstream source, and the same lists circulate outside Jewish sources.
**Questions:** Should Hineni show angels of the days or hours? If so, from which source, and with what description?

### 12. The permutations of the Name for each month
**Found:** each month has one of the twelve permutations of the Name, each from a verse (Arizal, Pri Etz Chaim; Bnei Yissaschar). We have not yet confirmed all twelve and their verses from the text.
**Questions:** Which source should we take them from? And how should the Name be shown on screen: in full, or with a substitute (for example ה׳ or with letters changed)?

### 13. Showing the Name on screen (already in Hineni)
**Now:** the Space view shows the six directions "sealed" with the three letters י־ה־ו in different orders (Sefer Yetzirah 1:13, the standard printed text), written out in full.
**Question:** Is it right to show these in full, or should they be written differently?

---

## D. Smaller points

14. **When the day begins:** the app says a Jewish day begins at sunset and notes that the time until nightfall is bein hashmashos. Is that the right way to say it?
15. **Hineni's own reflections:** "Today asks", "Focus" and "Be aware" are written by Hineni and labelled as such. Is that acceptable?
16. **The 6,000 years:** the app says each millennium is like one of the six days of creation (Ramban, Bereishis 2:3) and that we are in the sixth, "the world's Friday". Is that a fair way to put it?
